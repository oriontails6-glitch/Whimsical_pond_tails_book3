# Whimsical Pond Tails — Book Three (Flipbook)

A realistic page-curl flipbook for **"Whimsical Pond Tails — Book Three: Hatch Me If You Can"**, built with plain HTML/CSS/JS (no build step needed) so it can be hosted straight from GitHub Pages or opened directly in any browser.

## 📁 Folder Structure
```
book3/
├── index.html       ← complete book: cover, 10 story pages, back cover
├── style.css        ← dreamy whimsical storybook styling & mobile responsiveness
├── script.js        ← initializes realistic page-curl effect (StPageFlip)
├── images/          ← 10 story illustrations (page1.png to page10.png)
└── README.md
```

## 📖 Story Overview
1. **Page 1: The Egg Refuses to Clock In (Meet MiniGracie!)** — The egg doesn't hatch; MiniGracie demands cookies, her lawyer, and the manager from inside the shell!
2. **Page 2: Papa Mo’s Whirlwind Green Card Wedding** — Papa Mo proposes to Mama Duck with a bottle cap ring and a green card clause excluding Nobu!
3. **Page 3: The Birth of Ghost Nobu & The Memorial** — Nobu puts on a white sheet to haunt Papa Mo forever; Gracie holds a 5-crumb memorial service.
4. **Page 4: Papa Do’s Executive Return** — Papa Do cancels his vacation, takes over the dock with his mafia desk, and demands protection tax from Papa Mo!
5. **Page 5: All Seven Toddler Dragons Debut!** — All 7 toddler dragons rain down from the sky with permits, tantrums, and fireworks!
6. **Page 6: Emiliano’s Campaign & The Mustache Stakeout** — Emiliano campaigns for favorite sibling with free roasted cactus, while Papa Mo plots in fake mustaches!
7. **Page 7: Grandpa Iska’s Muddy Parade (With Fish!)** — Grandpa Iska leads the walk to school, and somehow every duck and fish gets covered in mud!
8. **Page 8: Aunt Polleh’s Vibrating Espresso Pellets** — Aunt Polleh dishes out hyper-caffeinated pellets that vibrate the whole pond at 10,000 RPM!
9. **Page 9: The Nursery Meltdown & Her Grace Koyal Expels Orion!** — Walking desks, dragon chaos, and Principal Koyal calmly expels Orion for excessive overachievement!
10. **Page 10: The Quiet Pond & Ghost Nobu’s Spooky Warning** — Orion writes at her new pond, while Ghost Nobu warns Papa Mo of Halloween in Book Four!

## 🚀 How to Host on GitHub Pages
1. Push this `book3` folder to a GitHub repository: e.g. `Whimsical_pond_tails_book3`.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` (root) and click **Save**.
4. Your flipbook will be live instantly!
